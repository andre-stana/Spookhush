On your windows

Leaves, embers & glowing pumpkins.

Autumn leaves and smoldering embers gather on the tops of your windows and the Dock, with flickering Jack-o'-lanterns peeking out of deeper piles. Drag a window and its leaves slide off; close it and mini-pumpkins drop and burst into sparks below.

Autumn Fall

Interactive menu: try Heavy, click Clear Leaves to summon an eerie Halloween wind gust, or toggle Lantern Light & Sound Effects.

What the night does

Drifts
Crimson, burnt-orange and golden autumn leaves tumble in 3D alongside glowing embers, violet sparks and carved mini Jack-o'-lanterns.
Piles
Leaves and smoldering ash pile up along the bottom of your screen, on top of your windows and on the Dock, with glowing pumpkins nestled inside deeper drifts.
Glows
Your cursor carries a warm flickering Halloween lantern that illuminates falling leaves and leaf piles as it passes near them.
Crackles
Zero-bloat procedural audio synthesizes dry leaf rustles and ember crackles when you sweep a drift, hollow pumpkin thuds when a window drops its pile, and an eerie wind howl when you clear the screen.
Hides
Screenshots and screen sharing show your clean desktop without the haunt, unless you turn on Show in Screenshots.
Rests
Lightweight native Metal & Direct3D 11 rendering. It pauses automatically while your screen sleeps, locks or is covered.
